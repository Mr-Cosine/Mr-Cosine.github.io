Wearable Exoskeleton System for Grip Assistance

2025  ·  Personal & Team

Goal: Design and prototype a 3D-printed wearable hand exoskeleton for users with limited grip strength, targeting actuation under 0.5s, a 1kg hold capacity, and comfort during continuous wear.

Description: Developed full SolidWorks assemblies integrating motors, batteries, and cable routing with anatomical fit geometry, resolving interference conflicts across 17 components. Outcome: Delivered a functional wearable prototype meeting all design targets — sub-0.5s actuation, 1kg load handling, and comfortable continuous wear — validated through hardware testing and iterative refinement.
