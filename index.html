PROJECTS

INTEGRATED

Wearable Exoskeleton System for Grip Assistance

Goal: Design and prototype a 3D-printed wearable hand exoskeleton for users with limited grip strength, targeting actuation under 0.5s, a 1kg hold capacity, and comfort during continuous wear.

Description: Developed full SolidWorks assemblies integrating motors, batteries, and cable routing with anatomical fit geometry, resolving interference conflicts across 17 components. Optimized geometries through 9 iterative 3D-print prototype cycles for comfort, performance, and robustness. Validated functionality using a breadboard control circuit powered by Arduino.

Outcome: Delivered a functional wearable prototype meeting all design targets — sub-0.5s actuation, 1kg load handling, and comfortable continuous wear — validated through hardware testing and iterative refinement.

SOFTWARE DEVELOPPING

Sci-Helper: Integrated Highschool Science Writing Utilities

Goal: Build a browser extension to speed up the process of writing scientific formulas and special characters in Google Docs.

Description: Developed a Firefox extension in JavaScript that provides hotkey-triggered insertion of superscripts, subscripts, Greek letters, and mathematical operators. All hotkeys are activated via sticky toggle buttons, making the workflow fast and intuitive for science students and researchers.

Outcome: A fully functional, published Firefox extension that streamlines formula writing in Google Docs — eliminating the need to manually search for special characters.
