Wearable Exoskeleton System for Grip Assistance

2025  ·  Personal & Team

Problem: Users with limited grip strength lack affordable, accessible assistive devices — existing solutions are either too expensive, too bulky, or not customizable to individual anatomy.

Solution: Led mechanical design within a multidisciplinary team to build a 3D-printed wearable exoskeleton from scratch — integrating motors, batteries, and cable routing with the mechanical assembly. Outcome: Delivered a functional prototype meeting all design targets, and comfortable continuous wear — on a minimal budget within $300.
