Wearable Exoskeleton System for Grip Assistance

2025  ·  Personal & Team

Problem: People with limited grip strength lack affordable assistive devices — what exists is expensive, bulky, or impossible to fit to an individual hand.

Solution: Led mechanical design in a multidisciplinary team, building a 3D-printed wearable exoskeleton from scratch — motors, batteries and cable routing packed into geometry shaped around a hand. 40+ print-and-test cycles, most of the design decisions made by printing a part and finding out it did not fit or poorly performs. Validated with a breadboard Arduino control circuit.

Outcome: A working prototype hitting every design target — actuation under 0.5 s, 1 kg hold, comfortable in continuous wear — for under $300 total.

Skills demonstrated: Iterative design, Design for additive manufacturing, 3D printing, Arduino development, C++

PDF Marker: PDF batch merging & keyword tagging tool

2026  ·  Work @ Vallourec

Problem: Production approval requires marking specific fields in PDFs that reviewers need to see. The documents are long, dense, and often being scanned rather than digital text, so finding the fields by eye is slow and easy to get wrong.

Solution: A desktop application that merges a batch of PDFs, runs OCR over the pages, rebuilds paragraphs out of the loose text regions OCR returns, and draws a red box around every paragraph containing the keyword. Rebuilding the paragraphs is the part that matter, as a phrase split across two detections never matches otherwise. In the demo, try the leniency control and watch the paragraphs break in both directions.

Skills demonstrated: Self-directed learning, algorithm design, desktop application architecture, JavaScript, Python.
